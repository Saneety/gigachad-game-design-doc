Ограничения: №1–2 сохранили больше мелкой растительной детализации и дополнительных цветов жидкости, чем требует анализ. №3 ближе к крупному пикселю, но точная сетка 16×16/×4 генерацией не гарантируется. ## Светлая читаемая ночь

![[night-01-moonlit.png]]

### Промпт

```text
Use case: stylized-concept, reference-guided game fake screenshot iteration. Produce ONE full 16:9 pixel-art GAMEPLAY screenshot, no comparison panels. Input image 1 is a secondary reference for rich forest and warm brass materials. Input image 2 is the PRIMARY reference for composition, functional clarity, simpler sprite forms, readable manufacturing. Reinterpret this SAME scene at NIGHT applying the new art direction below. Preserve gameplay meaning and arrangement: upper left ore outcrop with exposed mining drill; ore belt goes to stone furnace at middle left; silver ingots go east to central exposed mechanical press/rollers; distinct toothed gears go right and then down to storage crates; upper middle glass alchemical vessel with copper pipes; right side modular stone altar, cyan crystal center and four peripheral functional elements; one small tool-carrying armored adventurer near lower center; framing forest and small water edge at lower left. Keep routes unobstructed and actual machine belt ports connected. NO new production chains, new buildings or new mechanics.

CRITICAL NEW ART DIRECTION: significantly simplify source images' excessive fine detail. Crisp, intentional, large square pixel clusters, visual target 16x16 ART-pixel ground tiles enlarged 4x, each machine about 2x2 or 3x3 tiles. Consistent sprite pixel density, no high-resolution painted microtexture, no realistic 3D. Top-down WITH shallow front faces, orthographic, no perspective or diagonal isometric: bright top plane, front face 2-3 values darker, 1-2 art-pixel contact-occlusion strip. More rectangular/blocky forms, no puffy cartoons. Each mechanism has ONE dominant recognizable moving part, visible process opening, restrained steam/sparks, pipes or cables, supporting frame. 3-4 shade ramps per material, colored dark outlines not black. Brass ochre to gold to tiny cream highlight; cool steel. Copper cables and pipes use sparse large clamps, not tiny ornament. Quiet ground and broad leaf clusters; lush nature at the edges, NO decorative clutter on production grid. No tents, ornate banners, extra torches or wildlife distractions. Simplify HUD too: narrow unobtrusive metal-framed bars upper left and eight tool slots bottom center, numeric shortcuts only, no labels.

NIGHT LIGHTING: clearly night, cool blue-violet forest shadows but ground, routes, ore, player and unlit edges stay visible (roughly 25-35% ambient floor). Warm brass/wood machines stand out from cool biome without globally recoloring resource identities. Soft localized 2D light gradients over SHARP PIXEL sprites, subtle bloom ONLY on actual emissive fire, hot metal, magic crystal and contained alchemical liquid. Each active machine has a tiny functional process light; no decorative random glow speckles, no glowing outlines on every object. Furnace opening casts orange light onto ground in front, adjacent ingots and nearby press face; the cyan altar crystal casts an identifiable separate cyan pool on platform, pedestal inward faces and nearby ground. Cast shadows point away from each source. Ordinary ore is matte blue-grey irregular lumps, ingots silver rectangles, gears ochre toothed rings, all large and evenly spaced on dark conveyor with subtle chevrons and small brass port markers; resources never emit cyan light. One magic accent ONLY cyan (provisional), no rainbow alchemical liquids. Do not drown half the screen in black, overexpose crystal, blur sprites, add cinematic depth of field, text, titles or watermarks. Keep all machinery and resources readable at small viewing scale. HYPOTHESIS 1 — MOONLIT READABILITY: Closest evolution of reference 2. Square sturdy machine bodies, clear moderately chunky sprites, extremely legible silhouettes and sparse texture. Ambient at upper end around 35%, slate-blue and muted teal trees visibly sculpted by cool moonlight. Small warm furnace pool and similarly sized cyan altar pool are distinct but restrained; alchemy is a small distant cyan accent. Forest richness from reference 1 expressed as a few big canopy clusters. Balanced quiet midnight workshop where you could comfortably track every item without additional lamps. Brightest pixels confined to furnace core and crystal facets, no sky visible.
```
